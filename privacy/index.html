Stepland

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Stepland reads your steps and flights climbed from Apple Health to build your island. That information stays on your iPhone. We do not collect it, and we could not see it if we wanted to.

What the app reads

With your permission, Stepland reads two things from Apple Health:

  • Steps, as daily totals.
  • Flights Climbed, as daily totals.

It also reads the names of the devices and apps that recorded those steps, such as your iPhone or a watch's app, so that Settings can show you where your steps come from.

The app can only read. It never writes to or changes anything in Apple Health, and it reads nothing else from it.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Everything the app reads is used on your iPhone to draw your island and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Stepland has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Your Health information is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties.

What is kept on your iPhone

The app saves a few things on your device so that it works between visits: the avatar you chose, pictures of your past islands, what your island looked like when you last opened the app, and which seasonal objects you have earned. These stay on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Stepland Pro

Pro is a subscription sold through Apple's App Store. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple gives us sales totals that do not identify you.

Your choices

  • Turn off Health access at any time in the iPhone's Settings app, under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Stepland. The app will simply have no steps to build with.
  • Delete everything the app has saved by deleting the app.
  • Manage or cancel Pro in your Apple Account's subscriptions, or from Settings in the app.

Children

Stepland is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
