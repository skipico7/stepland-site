Stepland

Every step you take builds your island.

Stepland is an iPhone game that turns the walking you already do into a small world of your own.

  • Steps build land. Every 2,000 steps adds a piece to your island.
  • Flights build mountains. The stairs you climb raise them, up to a snowcapped peak.
  • A fresh island every Monday. Each week starts as open sea, and past islands are kept.

Your steps come from Apple Health and never leave your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
