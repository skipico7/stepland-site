My island is empty, or smaller than it should be

Stepland builds from the steps in Apple Health. Open the iPhone's Settings app, choose Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Stepland, and make sure both Steps and Flights Climbed are switched on. Your island catches up the next time you open the app.

The app shows fewer steps than my watch

Apple Health counts one device at a time. If you wear a Garmin or another watch, Health may be counting your iPhone's steps and not the watch's. In Stepland, open Settings to see where this week's steps come from. To count the watch:

  • In your watch's own app, allow it to write Steps and Flights Climbed to Apple Health.
  • In the Health app, open Steps, then Data Sources & Access. Tap Edit and drag your watch's app to the top. Do the same for Flights Climbed.

Where did my island go?

Every Monday the week starts again as open sea. Last week's island is kept under History.

Stepland Pro

  • Cancel or change your plan from Settings in the app, with Manage subscription, or in your Apple Account's subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
